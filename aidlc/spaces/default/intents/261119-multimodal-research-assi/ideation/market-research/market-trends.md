## What the retrieved pages support

Glean, Gemini Enterprise, Perplexity Enterprise, and Amazon Q Business describe grounded enterprise answers. Citation evidence differs by product. Glean's citation guide, Perplexity's internal-knowledge article, Amazon Q Business web-experience citations, and Gemini Deep Research source-cited reports are VERIFIED at the URLs in `competitive-analysis.md`. Microsoft documents web-search query citations in Copilot Chat only. Those citations are not available in the Copilot pane inside a Microsoft 365 app, and the pricing page does not state citations for enterprise-data answers. Amazon Q Business is no longer open to new customers. The pricing page says existing customers can continue. These are product-page facts, not a measured adoption rate. Market size, TAM, SAM, SOM, customer counts, and revenue are UNKNOWN, as Question 7 requires.

Optional or selectable web evidence is VERIFIED for Microsoft Copilot web search, Gemini Web Grounding, and Perplexity's Web, Org Files, both, or neither selector. Amazon Q Business has a separate admin control: responses can use enterprise data only, or enterprise data plus model knowledge. Model knowledge is not live web evidence. Amazon Q's web crawler indexes HTTPS sites. It is a connector, not proof of live web search on every request. ChatGPT Enterprise's external-web behavior is UNKNOWN from the pages retrieved. Perplexity's 500-file organization repository conflicts with the plan guide's retirement notice and is not a verified current entitlement.

Permission-aware access or enterprise administration is VERIFIED on the Glean, Microsoft, Google Cloud, OpenAI, and AWS pages cited in `competitive-analysis.md`. That does not verify multi-tenant isolation for this product's future tenants. Those vendors describe their own customer boundaries.

Image handling is uneven. Glean lists image understanding. Amazon Q Business lists image processing and image files. Gemini lists media generation. Perplexity's workshop page says PNG and JPEG are not Internal Knowledge Search types. Microsoft image retrieval is UNKNOWN. None of these findings measures retrieval quality for this product. The approved Recall@5 baselines remain unmeasured planning targets in `intent-statement.md`.

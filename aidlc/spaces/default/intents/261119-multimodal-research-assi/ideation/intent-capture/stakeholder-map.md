## Decision-makers vs. influencers

The Product Owner decides business scope and priority. [Q7]

The Enterprise Architect is accountable for architecture decisions. [Q7]

The AI Engineering Lead is accountable for agent implementation. [Q7]

The Security/Compliance Reviewer must agree security and compliance controls. [Q7]

The QA Lead owns verification evidence. [Q7]

The Release Approver authorizes production. [Q7]

The recorded answers name those accountabilities. They do not name any additional influencer. [Q7]

## Communication requirements

AI-DLC artifacts remain the approval authority. [Q8]

Checkpoint reporting also shows lifecycle status, traceability, work-package status, automated verification, security and quality gates, risks, dependencies, assumptions, infrastructure and model cost, and production readiness. [Q8]

GitHub Projects and the Excel workbook do not approve stages. [Q8]

UI-02 is the proposed product surface for that visibility, and it reads actual lifecycle artifacts and verification evidence. [Q8]
